# NexHiro

NexHiro is the agentic AI recruiter that turns your hiring pipeline into an autonomous engine.

Meet Hiro, one AI with five modes that source candidates, send outreach, screen over voice calls, and flag who to interview next. All running 24/7, so you wake up to shortlists instead of scrolling profiles.

## Hiro Modes

- **Hiro:Seek** - finds and scores candidates
- **Hiro:Reach** - messages and follows up
- **Hiro:Voice** - screens over the phone
- **Hiro:Next** - tells you who to act on
- **Hiro:Brain** - your recruiter copilot
